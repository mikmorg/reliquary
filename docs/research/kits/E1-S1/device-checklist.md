# E1 census: device and person checklist (paper form)

Fill in one **device page** for each device the person uses, including phones and tablets and
managed devices. The census script does not run on phones or managed devices, so fill in their
pages by looking at Settings together or by asking. Fill in one **person page** per person.

The fields follow the E1 note §4. Rules:

- Pseudonyms only (P3, P3-D1 …).
- No file, folder, album or account names, no e-mail addresses, no serial numbers, no places.
- Sizes are written as shown on screen and are binned at export (H3 R3).
- These pages stay with the private-store papers.
