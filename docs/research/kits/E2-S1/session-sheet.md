## 1. Introduction (read aloud, word for word)

> "Thank you for helping. Today we are testing an early design of the app, not you. You can't do
> anything wrong. If something is hard, that is exactly what we need to find out. I'll mostly stay
> quiet, because I want to see how it works when you're on your own at home. Please think aloud if
> you can: say what you're looking at and what you're trying to do. You can stop at any time."

Scripted replies only:
- "What do I do?" → "Please do what you would do if you were at home on your own."
- "Is this right?" → "What do you think?"
- "Can I click this?" → "Do whatever you would do at home."

### SUS (once, at the end)

1 = Strongly disagree, 5 = Strongly agree. "The system" means the app you just used.

| # | Statement | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I think that I would like to use this system frequently. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 2 | I found the system unnecessarily complex. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 3 | I thought the system was easy to use. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 4 | I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this system. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 5 | I found the various functions in this system were well integrated. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 6 | I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 7 | I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 8 | I found the system very cumbersome to use. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 9 | I felt very confident using the system. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 10 | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

**Scoring (facilitator, afterwards):** odd items score − 1; even items 5 − score; add the ten
values and multiply by 2.5. The result is 0–100 and is not a percentage. SUS score: ______

Note: item 1 ("frequently") fits a backup app poorly, since the app is meant to run unattended.
Keep the standard wording so the score stays comparable, and record any comment about it.
