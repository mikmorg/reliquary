# Consent addendum for the E1 interview (read after H3 §9)

H3's consent script (`docs/research/h3-research-data-governance.md` §9) covers the census and
labelling. This addendum adds the interview, the neutral facilitator and note-taking. It is a
draft: the owner approves it together with H3 §9 (H3 owner action 7). It is not legal advice.

> **Talking with you**
>
> I'd also like to ask you some questions about how you keep your photos and papers, and about
> times things went wrong. There are no right answers. I'm not testing you.
>
> **Who asks the questions:** [Owner], or [facilitator: relationship only, e.g. "a friend of
> Owner's who isn't family"]. If [facilitator] asks, [Owner] will leave the room for some
> questions, so you can speak freely.
>
> **Notes:** we write notes on paper. The notes describe the *kind* of thing ("holiday photos"),
> never names of files, people or places. ☐ We would also like to make an **audio recording**,
> only to check our notes. It stays on [Owner]'s home server, nobody else hears it, it is never put
> into an AI tool, and it is deleted within 90 days. Say no to the recording and we will just take
> notes.
>
> **What leaves:** only counts and themes, for example "4 of 7 people had lost photos when
> changing phones". You appear as "P3", never by name.
>
> **You can skip any question, stop at any time, or ask us to delete your notes.**
